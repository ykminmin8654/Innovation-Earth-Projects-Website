How they're hosted

One domain, many tools

Every product lives on its own subdomain of innovationearthprojects.org, managed from the Admin panel.

  1. 1

    Create the DNS record

    Add a CNAME record for your tool, e.g. toolname → username.github.io, so toolname.innovationearthprojects.org points at the tool's repository (which has its own CNAME file).

  2. 2

    Publish in Admin

    Open the Admin panel, create a Product, and paste the full URL (https://toolname.innovationearthprojects.org) into the URL field.

  3. 3

    It appears here

    Once the CMS saves and the site rebuilds, the card shows up in the grid above with a live status badge and an “Open” button.
