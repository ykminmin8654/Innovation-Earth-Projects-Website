Last updated: October 19, 2026

1. Who we are

Innovation Earth Projects ("we", "us", "our") is a student-run studio (Innovation Earth Projects LLC, Utah, USA) that provides free software tools at innovationearthprojects.org. Questions about this policy? Email us at InnovationEarthProjects@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

We keep collection to the absolute minimum needed to run the site:

  • Contact form messages. When you use our contact form we collect your name, email address, chosen topic, and message so we can reply to you.
  • Bot-protection data. We use Cloudflare Turnstile on our forms. Cloudflare processes technical data such as your IP address and browser signals to determine whether a request is automated.
  • Basic server logs. Our hosting providers (GitHub Pages and Cloudflare Workers) automatically record standard technical logs such as request timestamps and IP addresses. We do not access these logs to identify individuals.

We do not collect payment information, do not require accounts or sign-ups, and do not sell or rent your personal data to anyone — ever.

3. Cookies and local storage

This site does not use tracking cookies and does not run advertising or third-party analytics trackers. We only use two kinds of small local data:

  • "Strictly necessary" storage: a single entry in your browser's local storage (iep_cookie_consent) that remembers your cookie-banner choice so the banner does not reappear on every visit.
  • Cloudflare Turnstile cookies: set by Cloudflare when you interact with the human-verification checkbox on our forms, purely to prevent spam and abuse.

You can clear this data at any time through your browser settings; the site will keep working either way.

4. How we use your information

  • To respond to messages you send through the contact form.
  • To protect the site from spam, bots, and abuse.
  • To improve our tools and content.

5. Who we share information with

We share data only with the service providers that help us operate the site: GitHub (hosting), Cloudflare (worker API and bot protection), and Formspree (contact-form delivery to our inbox). Each of these companies processes data under its own privacy policy, and only to the extent needed to provide the service.

6. Children's privacy

Our tools are designed for students, including minors. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond what is necessary to answer a question they voluntarily send us. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has sent us information you would like removed, email us and we will delete it promptly.

7. Data retention & security

Contact-form messages are kept in our email inbox until we no longer need them to serve you, typically no more than 24 months. The site is served entirely over HTTPS. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, but we take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect what we hold.

8. Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR in the EU/EEA, CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal data, and to object to processing. Because we collect so little, the easiest way to exercise any of these rights is simply to email us at InnovationEarthProjects@gmail.com — we will respond within 30 days, at no cost.

9. Changes to this policy

If we materially change this policy we will update the "Last updated" date above. Continued use of the site after changes means you accept the updated policy.
