Questions, answered

How often will you actually email me?

Once a month, plus an occasional one-off when something big ships. If we can't say anything worth reading, we don't send anything.

What happens to my email address?

It goes straight to our newsletter provider (and our privacy policy covers the details). We never sell it, rent it, or hand it to advertisers.

Can I unsubscribe easily?

Yes — every issue has a one-click unsubscribe link in the footer. No "are you sure?" mazes.

Does it cost anything?

Nope. The newsletter is free, just like everything else we build.
