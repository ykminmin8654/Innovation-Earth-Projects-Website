Questions

Frequently asked

Do I need an account to use the tools?

No. Every tool works without signing up. We're rolling out optional accounts later for saving progress, syncing across devices, and team features — but nothing is gated.

Are all the tools free?

Most are. Some tools have a free tier and paid add-ons for advanced features. Each product page lists exactly what's free and what isn't.

Who's behind this?

A small group of students based in Utah. We started Innovation Earth Projects in 2024 and registered as a Utah LLC in 2025.

Can I suggest a tool?

Yes. Email us at InnovationEarthProjects@gmail.com and tell us what would help you. We build what students actually ask for.

Can I contribute?

We're a small team, but we're open to student contributors — designers, writers, engineers. Email us with what you're interested in.

Do you have a newsletter?

Yes — read past issues here. We post research notes, product updates, and studio writing.
