Questions

Frequently asked

Are these tools really free?

Yes. Every product we've shipped is free, and there are no plans to ever charge. We're a registered LLC and may add optional paid services someday, but the current tools stay free.

Do I need an account?

No. Every tool works without signup. Some tools may offer optional accounts later for saving progress, but nothing is gated.

Who's behind this?

A small group of students based in Utah. We started Innovation Earth Projects in 2024 and registered as an LLC in 2025.

How do you make money?

Right now, we don't. We may add optional paid features later (like advanced analytics or team plans), but the core tools will always be free.

Can I suggest a tool?

Absolutely. Email us at InnovationEarthProjects@gmail.com and tell us what would help you. We build what students actually ask for.

Can I contribute?

We're a small team, but we're always open to student contributors — designers, writers, engineers. Email us with what you're interested in.
